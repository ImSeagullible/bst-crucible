Combos Instinctual, Intentional and Infinitive
Combo Tier I
Instinctual Combo Any instinctual skill within ~7s of another · 1.2×
Fire any instinctual skill within about seven seconds of another instinctual skill of a different affinity — yours or your familiar's Trick. The combo-count multiplier escalates from 1.0× on the first hit to 3.0× on the fifth hit and beyond.
Combo Tier II
Intentional Combo Rampant→Durant→Eldritch→Volant→Rampant · 1.5× · grants Sunstrider / Moonstalker
Use an affinity while the previous one's Heart is active. Per the game's tooltips: Volant Heart → Rampant and Durant Heart → Eldritch complete a Sunstrider combo; Rampant Heart → Durant and Eldritch Heart → Volant complete a Moonstalker combo.
Combo Tier III
Infinitive Combo: Universality Sunstrider ↔ Moonstalker · 2.0× · Lv. 50
Use a Sunstrider skill (Brutal Rage, Risen Fall) while Moonstalker is active, or a Moonstalker skill (Hawkish Talons, Calamity) while Sunstrider is active, for the highest single-hit multiplier.
